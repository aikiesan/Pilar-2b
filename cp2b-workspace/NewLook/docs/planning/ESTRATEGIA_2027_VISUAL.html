4Can supply meet the biomethane mandate?

Each cell shows how much of the national mandate a given supply covers. Green means 100 % or more.

National gas use 54.4 M m³/d (Abegás 2025). The GHG target is converted to volume with CI 8.55 (biomethane, CNPE 4/2026) vs. 66 gCO₂e/MJ (natural gas, assumed, still to check). analysis/build_sp_mandate_coverage.py.
The resource never limits the mandate; realisation does. At 1 %, today's national production covers 71 %. SP's already-burned biogas alone would cover 470 %.

5Burn it for power, or upgrade it to biomethane?

Per Nm³ of CH₄: electricity revenue vs. molecule + environmental attribute (CGOB) − upgrading cost.

Biomethane advantage, R$ per m³ CH₄

Molecule R$ 2.25/m³ and attribute up to R$ 1.40 (Arsesp Mercado Cativo Verde, single press source, still to check). Upgrading €19/MWh ≈ R$ 1.19/m³. analysis/build_sp_route_conversion.py.

Attribute price needed for biomethane to win

Below ≈ R$ 281/MWh, biomethane wins with no attribute at all. The PLD sat at R$ 185–300 for most of Apr–Jul 2026. For self-consumed power, though, the CGOB price decides. So the mandate level sets how many plants switch.

7Carbon: methane leakage can erase the benefit

For substrates that avoid no methane (straw, vinasse), losses weigh much more than for manure.

Net reduction of a straw plant vs. total methane loss (tCO₂e/yr)

Worked example in docs/data/ESPEC_MODULO_GEE.md §5 (45,000 m³-scale plant, GWP 27). Leakage 2.5 % (Danish average), slip by technology: water scrubber ≤ 2 %, amine ≈ 0.04 %, membrane < 1 %.

Upgrading slip by technology (% of CH₄)

Leakage must be a first-line assumption in the GHG module and a measured value in the CGOB backing report (C1, C22). For a pig farm the same loss cuts only about 5 %.

8Digestate: mostly preserved value, not new revenue

Nutrients in SP digestate (Real scenario): N 381 kt, P₂O₅ 249 kt, K₂O 826 kt per year.

Nutrient value by today's fate (R$ mi/yr at import prices)

81 % already returns to soil (vinasse, filter cake, manure). Counting it as revenue double-counts. MSW is the truly new nutrient (R$ 328 mi). Straw removal is a hidden cost: 122 kt K₂O + 65 kt N leave the field (R$ 794 mi).

Digestate vs. crop demand in SP

PAM 2023 × indicative recommendation, pasture excluded. analysis/build_sp_digestate_nutrients.py. Vinasse alone covers ≈ 84 % of cane K₂O, which is why CETESB P4.231 sets doses by potassium.

10The strategy: three moves, in this order

The asset no one else has is the traceable chain: public data → 8 ingestion gates → canonical parameter with DOI → number on the map. First make it trustworthy, then make it decisive, then make it alive.

Move 1 · Nov 2026 – Feb 2027

Auditable credibility

"Can I cite this number in a report, a tender or a paper?"
  • Official-figures register (one value per indicator)
  • CP2b v5.1 opened (hash + DOI)
  • P10–P90 for all 645 municipalities
  • Validation against real plants (MAPE)
  • Quarantine of MG synthetic herd; fix calculator CO₂
Move 2 · Mar – Aug 2027

From biomass to project

"Where should we build, how big, and with what return?"
  • Logistics cost surface (OSRM)
  • Optimal siting (MCDA + location-allocation)
  • Techno-economic engine with a Brazilian CAPEX curve
  • GHG module (leakage, slip)
  • Digestate/NPK; gas grid supply–demand matching
Move 3 · Sep 2027 →

Live monitoring

"What is changing, and where is the realisation gap?"
  • 2008–2025 time series + year selector
  • Monthly seasonality
  • Remote sensing as data (harvest, plumes)
  • Observatory + alerts; live mandate panel
  • CGOB proof-of-backing pilot
