# PILAR-2b at FOSS4G Europe 2026

> **Lightning talk — Academic Track.** An open, reproducible, spatially-explicit
> platform for mapping the **mobilisable** biogas potential of São Paulo's 645
> municipalities. Live: **https://cp2b.unicamp.br/pilar2b** · License: **GPL-3.0**.

## One-line pitch

Theoretical biomass numbers overstate what's usable. **PILAR-2b** decomposes
theoretical potential into **realistically mobilisable** potential with an
explicit, literature-traceable correction methodology (**FDE**), and maps the
result per municipality — fully open source, on open government data.

## The headline result (São Paulo)

| | Energy potential |
|---|---|
| **Theoretical** | ~**124 TWh/yr** |
| **Mobilisable (FDE-corrected)** | ~**22 TWh/yr** — **17%** of theoretical |

The gap *is* the contribution: making the theoretical→technical step **explicit,
spatial, and reproducible** instead of a single hand-waved efficiency factor.

## Method in one paragraph

A **forward engine** computes biogas per residue stream as
`M_wet × TS × VS × BMP × FDE`, where **FDE = FC · FCo · FS · FL · η**
(collection efficiency · competing uses · seasonal availability · logistics ·
conversion). Every chemical/availability parameter lives in one canonical source
of truth (`feedstocks.yaml`) with ≥2 literature references each, propagated to
SQL/Python/TypeScript by a generator. Results carry **min/medio/max** uncertainty
bands (coupled scenarios). Substrates: sugarcane, soy, corn, coffee, citrus,
cattle/swine/poultry manure, urban solid waste, sewage.

## Why it's a FOSS4G story

- **Fully open stack:** FastAPI + **PostGIS** + **Leaflet**/React, browser-based —
  no desktop GIS required.
- **Open government data:** IBGE (PAM/PPM), MapBiomas, ANEEL, SNIS, … (see the
  [open-data landscape](./data/OPEN_DATA_API_LANDSCAPE.md)).
- **Reproducible & transferable:** single-source canonical parameters + a forward
  engine that generalises beyond SP (FAOSTAT × IPCC factors → other regions).
- **Standards-ready:** an additive OGC API layer (WMS/WFS) is prototyped for
  QGIS/partner interoperability.

## Where it's going

National (Brazil-wide) expansion · a **dynamic** (temporal) view — seasonality,
energy-price and waste-flow dynamics ([dynamics docs](./data/dynamics/)) ·
a **co-funded German–Brazilian** direction toward a global biogas-potential atlas.
